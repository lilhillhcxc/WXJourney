    WXJOURNEY

    Create custom weather forecasts by date and location.

    How to use:

    Add record(s) Enter a location using the [location picker]. Select a single date or date range in the [date picker]. Tap [✓] to create the record(s).
    Refresh forecast Use [↻] to update the forecasts. New forecast data is available at 00:00, 06:00, 12:00, and 18:00 UTC.
    Change Temperature Unit Tap [°F °C] to toggle the displayed unit.
    Edit a record Change the date of an existing record by tapping the [date picker]. Records automatically sort chronologically.
    Delete a record Tap [X] in an existing record to delete the record.
    Manage Journeys Tap [JOURNEYS] to open the Journeys screen where you can view, switch between, create, or delete saved journeys.
    Add New Journey On the Journeys screen, tap [NEW JOURNEY], enter a name for the new journey, and tap [✓] to save the journey.
    Save Daily Journey On the Journeys screen, tap [SAVE DAILY JOURNEY], enter a name, and tap [✓] to save a duplicate copy of your Daily Journey entries under a new name.
    Edit Journey Name Tap [✎] in the active journey to edit forecast details.
    Theme Selection Tap [❀] to cycle through the color themes.

    Forecast data is provided by the National Weather Service (NWS) and the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF).

    NWS date is available for 7 days from the current date and is used for locations within the U.S.

    ECMWF data is available for 15 days from the current date and is used for locations outside of the U.S. or when NWS data is not available.

    Forecast data automatically refreshes at 3AM, 9AM, 3PM, and 9PM UTC. No data loss occurs if the app attempts a refresh when no data connection is present.

    The temperatures displayed for AM, PM, and EV are the predicted temperatures for 8AM, 2PM, and 8PM respectively.

    The weather conditions displayed are for a range of six hours after the start of the respective time period.

    Tapping the location name in a record will open the Windy.com website for that location.
